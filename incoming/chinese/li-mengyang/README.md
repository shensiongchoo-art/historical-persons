# Li Mengyang (李梦阳)

**Person ID:** P_CHN_MING_LI_MENGYANG
**Dates:** 1473–1530
**Status:** ai_collected_unreviewed

## Quick Summary
Ming dynasty poet and literary theorist, leader of the Former Seven Masters (前七子). Known for his archaist literary manifesto advocating return to Han and Wei dynasty poetic models. Major figure in Ming literary history whose reaction against the Cabinet Style (台阁体) reshaped Chinese poetics.

## Key Facts
- Born 1473, Qingyang, Shaanxi (庆阳, 陕西 — later moved to Kaifeng)
- Jinshi 1493 (弘治六年)
- Known for literary archaism: "文必秦汉，诗必盛唐"
- Leader of Former Seven Masters (前七子)
- Served in Ministry of Revenue, impeached and imprisoned multiple times
- Died 1530
