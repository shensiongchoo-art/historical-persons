# 李攀龙 (Li Panlong)

**Person ID:** P_CHN_MING_LI_PANLONG

## Summary

Li Panlong (1514-1570) was a Ming dynasty writer, literary theorist, and official from Shandong. He co-led the Latter Seven Masters (后七子) literary movement with Wang Shizhen, advocating strict archaizing literary models. While his own poetry was poorly received, his anthologies _Gujin Shishan_ and _Tangshi Xuan_ were highly influential across East Asia.

- Courtesy name: 于鳞 (Yulin)

- Dismissed from office: 1557 (10-year hiatus)
- Died: 1570; Wang Shizhen succeeded as literary leader

## Literary Significance

Li was the more rigid, doctrinaire partner in the Li-Wang leadership. His anthologies established late Tang poets' canonical status. Contemporary critics found his own poetry derivative; later scholarship values his editorial contribution more than his creative output.
