# Li Dongyang — Person Profile

## Identity
- **Primary name (ZH):** 李东阳
- **Primary name (EN):** Li Dongyang
- **Courtesy name (ZH):** 宾之
- **Courtesy name (EN):** Binzhi
- **Art name (ZH):** 西涯
- **Art name (EN):** Xiya
- **Posthumous name:** 文正 (Wenzheng)

## Dates
- **Birth:** 1447 (正统十二年)
- **Death:** 1516 (正德十一年)
- **Era:** Ming Dynasty

## Geography
- **Birthplace:** Chaling, Huguang (茶陵, 湖广 — modern Hunan)
- **Primary residence:** Beijing (capital, official career)

## Career
- Jinshi: 1464 (天顺八年), age 18
- Hanlin Academy (翰林院) service
- Minister of Rites (礼部尚书)
- Grand Secretary (内阁首辅): 1505–1512
- Served under: Chenghua, Hongzhi, Zhengde emperors

## Literary Affiliation
- Founder of Chaling literary school (茶陵派)
- Key transitional figure between Cabinet style (台阁体) and Former Seven Masters (前七子)
- Authored extensive poetry, prose, and literary criticism
- Collected works: 李东阳集 (Li Dongyang ji), 怀麓堂集 (Huailutang ji)

## Historical Significance
- One of the most powerful Ming court officials of his era
- Shaped Ming literary culture for decades
- Served as jinshi examiner for multiple cohorts
- His literary dominance was the target of Li Mengyang's archaist reaction
