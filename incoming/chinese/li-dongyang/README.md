# Li Dongyang (李东阳)

**Person ID:** P_CHN_MING_LI_DONGYANG
**Dates:** 1447–1516
**Status:** ai_collected_unreviewed

## Quick Summary
Ming dynasty official, literary leader, and founder of the Chaling school (茶陵派). Jinshi of 1464 at age 18, rose to Grand Secretary (内阁首辅). Major figure in Ming court literary culture between the Cabinet style (台阁体) and the Former Seven Masters (前七子) archaist movement.

## Key Facts
- Born 1447, Chaling, Huguang (茶陵, 湖广, modern Hunan)
- Jinshi 1464 (天顺八年) at age 18
- Served under Chenghua, Hongzhi, Zhengde emperors
- Grand Secretary 1505–1512
- Founded Chaling literary school (茶陵派)
- Died 1516, posthumous name 文正
