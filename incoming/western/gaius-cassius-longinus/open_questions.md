# Gaius Cassius Longinus — Open Questions

1. Precise date and circumstances of his Epicurean conversion (48 BC, 46 BC, or earlier?) affect interpretation of his political motivations.
2. Location of his tomb on Thasos not archaeologically identified.
3. Whether Cassius truly believed the assassination would restore the Republic, or anticipated civil war.
4. Full reconstruction of his lost philosophical writings beyond what Cicero preserved.
5. Nature of his relationship with Brutus — genuine friendship or political alliance of convenience?
6. Why Decimus Brutus (the third ringleader) escaped Dante's Hell while Cassius and Brutus were placed in Satan's mouth.
7. Exact birth year uncertain (c. 86 BC).
