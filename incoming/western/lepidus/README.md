## Key Cautionary Notes

1. **Thin ancient sources**: Lepidus is less well documented than his triumviral colleagues Antony and Octavian. Many details of his career are reconstructed from scattered references.
2. **Later reputation as "weak"**: Sources from the Augustan period (Velleius Paterculus, Appian) portray Lepidus as the weakest triumvir. This framing reflects pro-Augustan bias — treat as reception, not neutral fact.
3. **Marginalization after 36 BCE**: Lepidus was stripped of his provinces but retained pontifex maximus until death. His later life is almost entirely undocumented.
4. **Birth year approximate**: ca. 89 BCE based on career chronology (magister equitum 46 BCE, consul 46 BCE). No ancient source gives explicit birth year.
5. **Relationship types**: Do NOT infer "friendship" or "alliance" with Caesar, Antony, or Octavian without explicit ancient attestation of personal bond. Use `documented_association` for professional/political relationships.
