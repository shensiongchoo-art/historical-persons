### Historical Disputes

10. **CLM010 (Magnus title origin):** Ancient sources differ on whether Sulla or Pompey's troops first used the title Magnus. A review of all ancient testimony (Plutarch, Appian, Dio) is needed.

11. **CLM039 (Pompey's generalship):** The modern scholarly consensus on Pompey as strategist rather than tactician should be cross-referenced against a broader survey of military historians.

## Research Gaps

12. **Pompey's wealth and economic impact:** The scale of Pompey's personal wealth, its sources (spoils, provincial administration, client kings), and its political effects deserve more detailed investigation.

13. **Relations with the publicani (tax farmers):** Pompey's relationship with the equestrian tax-farming companies, particularly after his eastern settlement, is not yet documented.

14. **Pompey's children after his death:** The careers of Gnaeus Pompeius the Younger and Sextus Pompey, who continued the civil war, are noted only in passing.

15. **Pompey's clients and freedmen:** The network of clients and freedmen that supported Pompey's political power is not yet documented.

16. **Archaeological evidence:** Modern archaeological work at sites associated with Pompey (Theatre of Pompey remains, eastern cities) should be surveyed.
