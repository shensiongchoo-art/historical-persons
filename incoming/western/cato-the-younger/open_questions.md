# Cato the Younger — Open Questions

1. Whether Cato would have accepted Caesar's pardon had he survived (implied as possible by Caesar's lament).
2. Extent of Cato's personal involvement in Cyprus embezzlement vs. Brutus's usurious lending.
3. Whether Cato voted for or against Curio's mutual disarmament proposal in 50 BC (sources contradictory).
4. Precise nature of his philosophical development: how deeply Stoic vs. politically performative.
5. Whether his brinkmanship was a cause of the civil war or whether he was genuinely surprised by Caesar's actions.
