# Open Questions — Marcus Licinius Crassus

1. **Exact birth year**: Traditionally given as ca. 115 BCE, but some sources suggest ca. 112 BCE. Needs verification against prosopographical evidence.

2. **Crassus' exact wealth**: Plutarch's figure of 7,100 talents needs modern economic contextualization. What was the composition of his fortune?

3. **Death at Carrhae**: The exact circumstances of Crassus' death vary between Plutarch, Cassius Dio, and other sources. Was he killed in a skirmish, executed after capture, or surrendered?

4. **Relationship with Caesar**: The extent of Crassus' financial support for Caesar and their political partnership needs more detailed source analysis.

5. **Crassus and the equestrian order**: Crassus' role as a bridge between senatorial and equestrian interests is often mentioned but needs better documentation.

6. **Military competence**: How to fairly evaluate Crassus as a military commander — competent against Spartacus but disastrous against Parthia?

7. **Portrait identification**: Are any surviving Roman portraits actually identifiable as Crassus?

8. **The Parthian campaign**: Detailed analysis of Crassus' strategic decisions and whether Carrhae was primarily a failure of intelligence, logistics, or diplomacy.
