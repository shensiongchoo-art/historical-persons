# Open Questions: Augustus / Octavian

## Unresolved Issues

| # | Question | Current Understanding | Evidence Needed |
|---|----------|----------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Constitutional nature of the Principate | Augustus held power through combinations of Republican magistracies, not a single office | Requires detailed legal-constitutional analysis of the settlements of 27 and 23 BCE |
| 2 | Livia's role in succession | Tiberius succeeded Augustus; whether Livia influenced this remains debated | Examination of Tacitus, Suetonius, and Cassius Dio accounts |
| 3 | Extent of Augustan propaganda | Res Gestae, coinage, and literature shaped the image of Augustus systematically | Critical analysis needed to separate verifiable fact from self-promotion |
| 4 | Augustus's health | Ancient sources describe frequent illnesses; some scholars suggest chronic conditions | Medical-historical analysis from ancient descriptions |
| 5 | Census of Quirinius chronology | Luke 2:1-2 mentions a census under Quirinius during Augustus's reign; chronological problems exist | Historical and textual analysis of the Gospel of Luke vs. Roman records |
| 6 | Exact nature of the 27 BCE settlement | What specific powers were transferred to Augustus vs. retained by the Senate? | Detailed reading of Cassius Dio Book 53 and scholarly reconstruction |
| 7 | Teutoburg Forest aftermath | How badly did the Varian disaster affect Augustus personally and strategically? | Suetonius's account of Augustus crying "Quinctilius Varus, give me back my legions!" |
| 8 | Relationship with Tiberius | Was Tiberius a reluctant heir? Why did Augustus exile Agrippa Postumus? | Tacitus Annals Book 1 and Suetonius Life of Tiberius |

## Recommended Follow-up Actions

- Consult critical editions of Suetonius *Divus Augustus*
- Read Cassius Dio Books 45-56 in a modern translation
- Examine *Res Gestae* critical edition (e.g., Cooley 2009)
- Consult modern scholarly syntheses: Syme *The Roman Revolution* (1939), Goldsworthy *Augustus* (2014), Southern *Augustus* (2014)
- Verify epigraphic evidence for titles, dates, and provincial administration
