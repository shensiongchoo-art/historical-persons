# Mark Antony (Marcus Antonius) — Open Questions

## Birth and Early Life

1. Exact birth year — 86 BCE or 83 BCE? Plutarch (Ant. 86.5) records age at death as both 53 and 56 in different manuscripts. Cicero's reference to his quaestorship timing points to 83 BCE. Resolution requires textual criticism of Plutarch manuscripts.

2. Antony's early youth and the "dissolute" tradition — how much of the Cicero-Plutarch narrative of a drunken, debt-ridden youth is historical versus polemical construction?

## Political and Military Questions

3. Antony's role in the Gallic Wars (54–50 BCE) — Caesar's Commentaries barely mention Antony; our only detailed source is Plutarch. What were Antony's specific military contributions?

4. The Lupercalia diadem offer (15 February 44 BCE) — was this a test arranged with Caesar, or did Antony act independently? Ancient sources are divided and reflect post-assassination narratives.

5. The Perusine War (41–40 BCE) — did Antony direct his brother Lucius and wife Fulvia, or did they act without his knowledge? Appian suggests complicity; other sources do not.

6. The Parthian campaign of 36 BCE — exact troop strength, route, and casualties need cross-referencing between Plutarch, Cassius Dio, and modern military analysis.

## Sources and Historiography

7. The "will" obtained from the Vestal Virgins (32 BCE) — was it genuine, forged, or selectively edited by Octavian? Ancient sources accept its authenticity; modern scholars are divided.

8. What were Augustus' lost Memoirs ("De Vita Sua," 13 books) and how extensively did they shape the anti-Antonian tradition? The memoirs do not survive but were used by Plutarch, Suetonius, and Appian.

9. How much of the surviving narrative of Antony comes from Asinius Pollio, a Caesarian and Antonian partisan who wrote a lost history of the civil wars? Pollio's version would have been more favorable to Antony but is now lost.

## Portraiture and Archaeology

10. The Vatican portrait bust traditionally identified as Mark Antony (Sala dei Busti, Inv. 592) — is the identification correct? Physiognomic comparison with verifiable coin portraits needs systematic analysis.

11. Were any spaces on the Dendera Temple left unfinished specifically for Antony's cartouche, as sometimes claimed?

12. What archaeological evidence exists for Antony's building program in Alexandria and the eastern provinces? Tacitus (Ann. 2.3) mentions Antony gave territories to Cleopatra but archaeological traces are sparse.

## Reception

13. The earliest post-Augustan rehabilitation of Antony — when did Roman writers begin to present a more balanced view? Plutarch (c. 100–110 CE) is already more nuanced than the Augustan poets.

14. Antony in non-Western historiography — how is Antony depicted in Egyptian/Arabic historical traditions, if at all?
