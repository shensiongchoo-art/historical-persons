## Key Points

1. **One claim = one fact**: No compound claims. Each claim expresses a single verifiable fact.
2. **Source calibration**: All claims calibrated to primary ancient sources where possible.
3. **Propaganda awareness**: Augustan propaganda fundamentally shaped the surviving ancient sources; legendary_notes.jsonl documents this issue.
4. **Reception marking**: Later interpretive traditions (Shakespeare, "dissolute youth," "Roman traitor" narrative) are marked as reception.
5. **Antony was never an emperor**: He was a triumvir and general of the late Republic.

## Notable Biographical Highlights

- Tribune of the Plebs whose veto triggered Caesar's crossing of the Rubicon (49 BCE)
- Caesar's Master of Horse and consular colleague (44 BCE)
- Delivered Caesar's funeral oration that turned Rome against the assassins
- Formed the Second Triumvirate with Octavian and Lepidus (43 BCE)
- Defeated the Liberators at Philippi (42 BCE)
- Alliance with Cleopatra VII (41–30 BCE)
- Defeated at Actium (31 BCE), died by suicide (30 BCE)
