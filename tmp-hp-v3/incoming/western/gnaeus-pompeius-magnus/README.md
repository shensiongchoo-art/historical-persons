## Key Cautionary Notes

1. **"First Triumvirate" is a modern historiographical label**, not an ancient constitutional office. Claims use reception-appropriate language.
2. **"Defender of the Republic" is a reception framing**, not a contemporary political position. Pompey's career was built on bypassing Republican norms.
3. **Classical sources are treated critically** — Plutarch wrote ~150 years after events; Caesar's *Civil War* is partisan; Cassius Dio is 3rd century. All marked with appropriate reliability levels.
4. **The "Magnus" cognomen has a disputed origin** — troops in Africa vs. Sulla's irony. Formal adoption by Pompey himself is the confirmed fact.
5. **All visual media is staging** — museum inventory numbers not verified.
6. **"Pompey's Pillar" in Alexandria is a confirmed misattribution** (built for Diocletian, 298–302 CE).
7. **BCE dates use explicit era/year_number/astronomical_year fields** per v2.5 rules, not negative integers.
