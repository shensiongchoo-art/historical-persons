## Uncertain Citations

### Testamentary Adoption — Legal Ambiguity
- The claim that Caesar's will created a testamentary adoption is **legally disputed** in modern scholarship. The will created a *condicio nominis ferendi* (condition of assuming the name), which was not equivalent to full *adrogatio* in Roman law.
- The *lex curiata* formalizing adoption was only passed in August 43 BCE after Octavian became consul.
- **Recommendation**: The source entry should note this legal ambiguity. Modern sources (Tatum 2024, Lindsay 2009, Goldsworthy 2014) discuss this.

### Augustus "First Emperor" — Reception Status
- No ancient source calls Augustus "first emperor." This is a **modern historiographical category**.
- The term "emperor" derives from his use of *imperator* as a permanent title, but he held no constitutional office called "emperor."
- **Recommendation**: Claims using "first emperor" should be explicitly marked as modern reception.
