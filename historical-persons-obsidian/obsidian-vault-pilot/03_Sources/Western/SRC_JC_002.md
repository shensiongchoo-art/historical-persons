## Notes

Caesar's account of the civil war against Pompey and the Senate (49–45 BCE). Written in the same third-person style as the Gallic War. Books 1–3 cover events from the crossing of the Rubicon through the Alexandrian War.

**Source caveat:** As with the Gallic War, Caesar presents events to justify his own actions. His crossing of the Rubicon is framed as a response to Senatorial provocation. The account of Pharsalus emphasizes Caesar's military skill while minimizing the controversy of fighting fellow Romans.

Loeb Classical Library vol. 39 (LCL 39) provides Latin text with facing English translation by A.G. Peskett. The Alexandrian War, African War, and Spanish War (continuations by other authors) are also in this volume.
