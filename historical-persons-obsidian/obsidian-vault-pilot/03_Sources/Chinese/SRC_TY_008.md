## Notes

The 正德姑苏志 (Zhengde-era Gusu Gazetteer) was compiled under Wang Ao (王鏊, 1450–1524), a contemporary of Tang Yin and fellow Suzhou literatus. First published in 1506 (正德元年), this is the earliest plausible edition of the Suzhou prefectural gazetteer.

Tang Yin's biography should be in the 人物志 (Biographies) or 文苑传 (Literary Biographies) section. Exact juan number needs verification.
