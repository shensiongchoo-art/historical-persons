## Notes

The standard English-language monograph on Tang Yin's painting career. Covers:
- Tang Yin's apprenticeship under Zhou Chen
- His landscape, figure, and bird-flower painting genres
- The synthesis of Southern Song academy style with literati painting conventions
- Tang Yin's place in Ming art history and the formation of the "Four Masters" canon
