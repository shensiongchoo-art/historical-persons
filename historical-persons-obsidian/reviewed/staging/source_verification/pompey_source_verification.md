## Verified Citations

### Plutarch, Life of Pompey
- **Ch. 1**: Family background and birth in Picenum. Confirmed.
- **Ch. 14**: First triumph (81/80 BCE) without holding formal magistracy. Confirmed — "an unprecedented request for one so young."
- **Ch. 25–28**: Campaign against the pirates (67 BCE) under *Lex Gabinia*. Confirmed. Specific citations: 26.2, 24.4-5, 28.2.
- **Ch. 30–45**: Eastern campaign against Mithridates VI (66–62 BCE). Confirmed. Specific citations: 30.1-5 (Lex Manilia), 32.1-3 (Lycus River), 32.3-7 (Mithridates' flight).
- **Ch. 46–47**: Formation of First Triumvirate (60 BCE) and marriage to Julia (59 BCE). Confirmed.
- **Ch. 68–73**: Battle of Pharsalus (48 BCE). Confirmed.
- **Ch. 74–80**: Flight to Egypt and assassination. Confirmed.

### Appian, Civil Wars
- **Book 1.80**: Pompey raised troops for Sulla (83 BCE). Confirmed for narrative but number of legions not independently verified from this passage.
- **Book 2.9**: First Triumvirate formation. needs_verification for exact section.
- **Book 3.82–99**: Battle of Pharsalus. Confirmed.
- **Book 3.103–104**: Pompey's death in Egypt. Confirmed. Note: Appian arrived after Pompey's death; death reported, not witnessed.

### Cassius Dio, Roman History
- **Books 36–37**: Mithridatic campaign and Eastern reorganization. Confirmed. Specific: 36.14.4, 36.17.1, 36.45-46, 36.47, 36.48-50, 36.54, 37.2-5.1, 37.11-14.2.
- **Book 42**: Pharsalus and death. needs_verification for exact chapters on death narrative.

### Caesar, Civil War
- **Book 3.82–99**: Campaign leading to Pharsalus. Confirmed — Caesar's contemporary account, explicitly biased in his favor.

### Pliny the Elder, Natural History
- **37.13**: Pompey's birth date (29 September 106 BCE). Confirmed — this is the source for the precise calendar date, not Plutarch.

## Uncertain Citations

### Cicero, Pro Lege Manilia — needs_verification
- Sections cited as 27–35 but require verification. The speech (66 BCE) argues for granting Pompey the Mithridatic command and does reference the pirate campaign's success, but exact section numbering needs cross-referencing with a critical edition.

### Plutarch, Life of Crassus — needs_verification
- Ch. 14 cited for First Triumvirate. Requires cross-referencing with a critical edition to confirm exact chapter and content.

### Florus, Epitome 2.13 — NOT "one of the most magnificent"
- The actual Florus passage (2.13.14) states something like: "Pompey could not bear an equal, nor Caesar a superior." There is NO "one of the most magnificent" in the verified text. If a claim in the package cites Florus for a superlative, this is **unsubstantiated** and should be downgraded or marked as needs_verification/reception.

### Valerius Maximus 6.2.8 — needs_verification
- "Adulescentulus carnifex" (teenage butcher) attributed here. Wikipedia confirms this attribution through modern scholarship (Collins 1953, Leach 1978) but the exact passage requires cross-referencing with a critical edition. Also cited as from Gnaeus Papirius Carbo or his supporters.

### Pompey raised "three" legions — needs_verification
- Plutarch's account mentions "a legion," not three. The number "three" may come from a different source or later synthesis. Needs cross-referencing with Appian and Cassius Dio.
