# Li Dongyang — Open Questions

1. **Chaling school composition:** Which specific writers constituted the Chaling school beyond Li Dongyang himself? The school is well-attested but its membership roster is less precisely defined than 前七子 or 吴中四才子.

2. **Relationship with Wu Kuan:** Were Wu Kuan and Li Dongyang personally close, or was their connection primarily through the jinshi cohort and Hanlin Academy service? 明史 documents their same-year jinshi status but does not describe personal friendship.

3. **Role in Liu Jin affair:** Li Dongyang's conduct during the eunuch Liu Jin's dominance (1505-1510) was controversial. Some sources suggest he accommodated Liu Jin to maintain stability; others defend him. Needs careful source review.

4. **Influence on Later Seven Masters (后七子):** The extent to which Li Dongyang's poetics influenced the Later Seven Masters (e.g., Li Panlong, Wang Shizhen) is debated.

5. **Missing sources:** SRC_LDY_003 (monograph on Li Dongyang and Chaling school) needs author and publisher verification.
