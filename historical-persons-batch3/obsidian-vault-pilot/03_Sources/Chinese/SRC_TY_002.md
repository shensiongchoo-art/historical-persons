## Notes

Modern typeset edition of Tang Yin's collected poetry and prose, published by Xiling Seal Engraver's Society Press (西泠印社出版社) in 2012 as part of the "中国古代书画家诗文集丛书" series.

A critical annotated edition also exists: 唐伯虎集笺注, annotated by 陈书良 and 周柳燕, published by 凤凰出版社 (Phoenix Publishing House), 2023, ISBN 9787550640467. This later annotated edition is preferred for scholarly citation.

As collected works by the subject himself, this is a primary source. However, collected works posthumously compiled may omit, rearrange, or sanitize content — exact provenance of the base text used for the 2012 edition needs verification.
