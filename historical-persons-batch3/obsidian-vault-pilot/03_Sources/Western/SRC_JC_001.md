## Notes

Caesar's own account of the Gallic Wars (58–50 BCE), written in clear third-person Latin prose. Books 1–7 cover the campaigns year by year; Book 8 was added by Aulus Hirtius after Caesar's death.

**Source caveat:** As autobiography written for a Roman political audience, the Commentarii are self-promotional. Caesar's account of battles, enemy strength, and his own motivations should be read critically. The text is invaluable as a primary source, but it is not neutral reportage.

Loeb Classical Library vol. 72 (LCL 72) provides Latin text with facing English translation by H.J. Edwards. Exact section references for specific claims need verification.
