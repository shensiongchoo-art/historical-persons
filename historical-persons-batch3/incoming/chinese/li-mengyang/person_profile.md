# Li Mengyang — Person Profile

## Identity
- **Primary name (ZH):** 李梦阳
- **Primary name (EN):** Li Mengyang
- **Courtesy name (ZH):** 献吉
- **Courtesy name (EN):** Xianji
- **Art name (ZH):** 空同
- **Art name (EN):** Kongtong

## Dates
- **Birth:** 1473 (成化九年)
- **Death:** 1530 (嘉靖九年)
- **Era:** Ming Dynasty

## Geography
- **Birthplace:** Qingyang, Shaanxi (庆阳, 陕西)
- **Later residence:** Kaifeng (开封)

## Career
- Jinshi: 1493 (弘治六年)
- Ministry of Revenue (户部) service
- Multiple impeachments and imprisonments
- Outspoken critic of eunuch power

## Literary Affiliation
- Leader of Former Seven Masters (前七子之首)
- Archaist manifesto: "文必秦汉，诗必盛唐"
- Author of 空同集 (Kongtong ji)
- Central figure in Li-He debate with He Jingming

## Historical Significance
- Redefined Ming literary standards through archaist movement
- His reaction against Cabinet Style and Li Dongyang's dominance shaped Ming poetics for generations
- The Former Seven Masters under his leadership became the most influential literary group of mid-Ming
