## Verified Citations

### Modern Academic Scholarship

**Ellen Johnston Laing**:
- "Problems in Reconstructing the Life of Qiu Ying." *Ars Orientalis* 29 (1999): 69–90. — **Confirmed**. This is the foundational English-language paper establishing that Qiu Ying left no writings of his own.
- "Qiu Ying's Delicate Style." *Artibus Asiae* 57, no. 1/2 (1997): 3–42. — **Confirmed**.
- "Qiu Ying's Other Patrons." *Journal of the American Oriental Society* 117, no. 4 (1997): 686–692. — **Confirmed**.
- "Sixteenth-Century Patterns of Art Patronage: Qiu Ying and the Xiang Family." *Journal of the American Oriental Society* 111, no. 1 (1991): 1–7. — **Confirmed**.
- "Ch'iu Ying's Three Patrons" (1979). — Published in *Ming Studies*. **Confirmed** to exist; exact volume/pages needs_verification.

**James Cahill**:
