# Cleopatra VII Philopator / 克娄巴特拉七世·菲洛帕托尔

## Summary

托勒密埃及最后一位实际统治者（前51-30年在位）。与凯撒和马克·安东尼结盟，凯撒里昂之母。在亚克兴海战（前31年）中被屋大维击败。其死亡标志着托勒密王朝终结，埃及并入罗马帝国版图。

Last active ruler of Ptolemaic Egypt (r. 51-30 BCE). Formed alliances with Julius Caesar and Mark Antony; mother of Caesarion. Defeated by Octavian at the Battle of Actium (31 BCE). Her death marked the end of the Ptolemaic dynasty and Egypt's absorption into the Roman Empire.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Cleopatra VII Philopator was born in early 69 BCE in Alexandria.
- Cleopatra died on 10 or 12 August 30 BCE in Alexandria.
- Cleopatra belonged to the Macedonian Greek Ptolemaic dynasty, descended from Ptolemy I, a general of Alexander the Great.
- Cleopatra co-ruled with her brother Ptolemy XIII from 51 BCE and was later driven from Alexandria in a power struggle.
- Cleopatra allied with Caesar upon his arrival in Egypt in 48 BCE and was restored to the throne.
- Cleopatra and Caesar had a son, Caesarion (Ptolemy XV Caesar).

### Disputed / Uncertain
- Multiple accounts of Cleopatra's manner of death exist: snake bite, poison, or other means; classical sources vary.

### Reception / Later Groupings (not formal organizations)
- Roman propaganda (especially Octavian's side) portrayed Cleopatra as an 'Oriental seductress' — this image should not be treated as objective historical assessment.
- Later literature, drama, and film (including Shakespeare's 'Antony and Cleopatra') have portrayed Cleopatra in romanticized or spectacularized terms.
