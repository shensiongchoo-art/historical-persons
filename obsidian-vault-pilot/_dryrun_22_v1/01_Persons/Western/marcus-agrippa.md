# Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa / 马库斯·维普萨尼乌斯·阿格里帕

## Summary

罗马将军、海军统帅和行政官，奥古斯都的亲密伙伴。亚克兴海战（前31年）的胜利者。主持罗马大规模公共工程（万神殿、引水渠、浴场）。娶奥古斯都之女尤利亚为妻，为卡利古拉之祖父、尼禄之曾外祖父。

Roman general, admiral, and administrator; close associate of Augustus. Victor at the Battle of Actium (31 BCE). Oversaw extensive public works in Rome (Pantheon, aqueducts, baths). Married to Augustus' daughter Julia; grandfather of Caligula and great-grandfather of Nero.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa was born around 64-63 BCE to a plebeian family.
- Agrippa died in March 12 BCE in Campania.
- Agrippa met Octavian (the future Augustus) as a young man in Apollonia and later became his most trusted military commander and political partner.
- Agrippa defeated Sextus Pompey at the naval Battle of Naulochus in 36 BCE.
- Agrippa commanded Octavian's fleet at the Battle of Actium in 31 BCE, defeating Antony and Cleopatra.
- Agrippa served as consul three times: in 37, 28, and 27 BCE.
