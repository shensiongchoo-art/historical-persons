# Marcus Aemilius Lepidus / 马库斯·埃米利乌斯·雷必达

## Summary

罗马共和国晚期政治家。曾任市政官（前49年）、凯撒的骑兵司令（前46–44年）、执政官（前46年），与安东尼和屋大维组成后三头同盟（前43–36年）。自前44年起任大祭司直至去世。前36年因试图夺取西西里而被屋大维剥夺行省，保留大祭司头衔但再无实权。古代文献记载甚薄；其「最弱三头」的名声反映了奥古斯都时代的政治偏见。

Roman politician of the late Republic. Praetor (49 BCE), magister equitum under Caesar (46–44 BCE), consul (46 BCE), member of the Second Triumvirate with Mark Antony and Octavian (43–36 BCE). Pontifex maximus from 44 BCE until his death. Stripped of his provinces by Octavian in 36 BCE after attempting to claim Sicily. Retained the pontificate but lived in political obscurity thereafter. Sources are thin; later reputation as the 'weakest' triumvir reflects Augustan-era bias.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Lepidus was born ca. 89 BCE into the patrician gens Aemilia, one of Rome's oldest families.
- His father was Marcus Aemilius Lepidus (consul 78 BCE), who led a failed revolt against the Sullan constitution and died in Sardinia.
- In 49 BCE, Lepidus served as praetor and sided with Caesar after the crossing of the Rubicon.
- In 46 BCE, Caesar appointed Lepidus magister equitum (Master of Horse); that same year Lepidus served as consul alongside Caesar.
- After Caesar's assassination in 44 BCE, Lepidus became pontifex maximus, holding the office for life.
- In November 43 BCE, Lepidus formed the Second Triumvirate with Mark Antony and Octavian, granted extraordinary powers for five years.
