# Gaius Julius Caesar / 盖乌斯·尤利乌斯·凯撒

## Summary

罗马将军、政治家和独裁官。征服高卢（前58-50年），内战中击败庞培（前49-45年），任终身独裁官至前44年被刺杀。著有《高卢战记》和《内战记》。其名号成为后世罗马皇帝头衔。

Roman general, statesman, and dictator. Conqueror of Gaul (58-50 BCE), victor in the Civil War against Pompey (49-45 BCE), and dictator perpetuo until his assassination in 44 BCE. Author of Commentarii de Bello Gallico and de Bello Civili. His name became the imperial title 'Caesar'.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Gaius Julius Caesar was born on 12 or 13 July 100 BCE in Rome.
- Caesar was assassinated on the Ides of March (15 March) 44 BCE in the Theatre of Pompey, Rome.
- Caesar belonged to the gens Julia, which claimed descent from the goddess Venus.
- In 60 BCE, Caesar formed an informal political alliance with Pompey and Crassus (later termed the 'First Triumvirate').
- Caesar served as consul in 59 BCE.
- Caesar governed Gaul from 58 to 50 BCE, during which he conquered the entirety of Gaul.
