# Shen Zhou / 沈周

## Summary

沈周（1427-1509），字启南，号石田，晚号白石翁，长洲（今苏州）人，明代吴门画派创始人，与文徵明、唐寅、仇英并称明四家。一生未仕，以诗书画名世。

Shen Zhou (1427-1509), courtesy name Qinan, pseudonym Shitian, was a founder of the Wu School of Ming dynasty painting, celebrated as one of the Four Masters of the Ming.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Shen Zhou was born in the second year of the Xuande era (1427).
- Shen Zhou died in the fourth year of the Zhengde era (1509) at age 83.
- Shen Zhou's courtesy name was Qinan, pseudonyms Shitian and Baishiweng.
- Shen Zhou was from Xiangcheng, Changzhou (modern Suzhou, Jiangsu).
- Shen Zhou came from a literati family; his grandfather Shen Cheng, uncle Shen Zhenji, and father Shen Hengji were all poets and painters who declined to sit for the civil examinations.
- Shen Zhou never held office, never sat for the civil examinations, and repeatedly declined official appointments.
