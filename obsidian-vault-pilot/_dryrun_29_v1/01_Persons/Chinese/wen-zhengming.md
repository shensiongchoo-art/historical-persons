# Wen Zhengming / 文徵明

## Summary

明代苏州文人、画家、书法家、诗人。屡试不第，后以岁贡生授翰林院待诏。沈周弟子，与唐寅、祝允明、徐祯卿合称“吴中四才子”，在后世美术史中与沈周、唐寅、仇英并列“明四家”。

Ming dynasty Suzhou literatus, painter, calligrapher, and poet. Repeatedly failed the metropolitan examination; later appointed Hanlin Academy Drafter by suigong. Student of Shen Zhou; grouped among the 'Four Talents of Wu' and, in later art history, the 'Four Masters of the Ming'.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Wen Zhengming was born in November 1470 in Changzhou County, Suzhou Prefecture.
- Wen Zhengming died in 1559 at the age of 90 sui.
- Wen Zhengming was originally named Wen Bi, with courtesy name Zhengming; he later used Zhengming as his primary name and adopted the courtesy name Zhengzhong, with the art name Hengshan Jushi.
- Wen Zhengming repeatedly attempted and failed both the provincial and metropolitan examinations.
- In 1523, Wen Zhengming was appointed Hanlin Academy Drafter (Daizhao) through the suigong route.
- Wen Zhengming resigned from the Hanlin Academy after a short tenure and returned home to devote himself to painting and calligraphy.
