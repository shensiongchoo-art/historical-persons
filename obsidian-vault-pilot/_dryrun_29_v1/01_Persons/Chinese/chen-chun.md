# Chen Chun / 陈淳

## Summary

明代画家、书法家、诗人。师从文徵明，后自创水墨写意风格，与徐渭并称'白阳青藤'。吴门画派重要成员。

Ming dynasty painter, calligrapher, and poet. Studied under Wen Zhengming before developing his own expressive ink-wash style. Paired with Xu Wei as 'Baiyang and Qingteng,' two great masters of freehand flower painting.
