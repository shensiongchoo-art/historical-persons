# Xu Zhenqing / 徐祯卿

## Summary

明代诗人、文学家。字昌谷，吴县人。弘治十八年进士，以诗名世，与唐寅、祝允明、文徵明合称“吴中四才子”。亦与李梦阳等被称为“前七子”之一。早卒，年仅三十三。

Ming dynasty poet and literary figure. Courtesy name Changgu, from Wu County. Jinshi of 1505; known for his poetry. Grouped among the 'Four Talents of Wu' and associated with the 'Former Seven Masters'. Died young at age 33.
