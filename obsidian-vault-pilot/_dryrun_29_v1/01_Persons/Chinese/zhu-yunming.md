# Zhu Yunming / 祝允明

## Summary

明代苏州书法家、诗人、文学家。字希哲，号枝山。以草书狂放闻名，与唐寅、文徵明、徐祯卿合称“吴中四才子”。

Ming dynasty Suzhou calligrapher, poet, and writer. Courtesy name Xizhe, art name Zhishan. Known for his bold cursive calligraphy; grouped among the 'Four Talents of Wu' with Tang Yin, Wen Zhengming, and Xu Zhenqing.

- Zhu Yunming was born in 1461 in Changzhou County, Suzhou Prefecture.
- Zhu Yunming died in 1527.
- Zhu Yunming, courtesy name Xizhe, used the art names Zhishan and Zhizhi Sheng.
