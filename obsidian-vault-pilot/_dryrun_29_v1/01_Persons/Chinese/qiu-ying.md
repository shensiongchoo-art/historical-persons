# Qiu Ying / 仇英

## Summary

明代职业画家，工笔青绿山水与仕女人物画代表人物，后世列为明四家之一。出身漆工，拜周臣为师，得项元汴等藏家供养。

Ming dynasty professional painter renowned for meticulous gongbi blue-and-green landscapes and figure paintings; posthumously classified as one of the Four Masters of the Ming. Rose from lacquer artisan origins, studied under Zhou Chen, and was patronized by leading collectors including Xiang Yuanbian.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Qiu Ying's courtesy name was Shifu (also written Shifu), and his art name was Shizhou.
- Qiu Ying was born in Taicang (present-day Taicang, Jiangsu Province) and later moved to Suzhou.
- Qiu Ying was born to a commoner family and initially worked as a lacquer artisan.
- Qiu Ying did not participate in the civil service examinations. He was a professional painter, socially distinct from the literati painters Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming, and Tang Yin.
- Ellen J. Laing (1999) established that Qiu Ying left no writings about himself; all biographical data comes from external sources.
- Qiu Ying studied painting under Zhou Chen (ca. 1450–1535), the leading professional painter in Suzhou.

### Disputed / Uncertain
- *(none flagged disputed)*

### Reception / Later Groupings (not formal organizations)
- In later art history, Qiu Ying is grouped with Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming, and Tang Yin as one of the Four Masters of the Ming (also called the Four Masters of Wu). This classification was consolidated by Dong Qichang (1555–1636) after all four had died.
- The "Four Masters of the Ming" is a later art-historical classification label, not a formal group that existed during the four painters' lifetimes.
- Dong Qichang assessed that Qiu Ying's mastery of color was comparable to the great masters of the Tang and Song dynasties.
- Qiu Ying's blue-and-green landscapes were so popular that Suzhou workshops produced extensive forgeries under his name during the late Ming and early Qing periods.
- Qiu Ying was known alongside Zhao Mengfu and Tang Yin as a painter of erotic spring paintings during the Ming period.

## Open Questions

### 生卒年
1. 仇英的确切生卒年尚无定论。学界范围涵盖1482–1505年（生年）至1552年（卒年），跨度逾二十年。需要从明代藏家记录、墓碑、方志中寻找更多证据以精确化。
2. 若能确证周凤来寓居期间（约1537–1543年）的起止年份，可为仇英生平提供更可靠的时间锚点。

### 师承关系
3. 仇英拜周臣为师的年份与具体经过不详。周臣的生卒年（约1450–1535年）本身也不精确。
4. 仇英与文徵明合作始于何时？除《湘君湘夫人图》题跋中的记录外，是否有其他合作或交流的直接证据（书信、题画诗、诗文提及）？

### 作品归属
5. 天津博物馆所藏《桃源仙境图》是否为仇英真迹？需要查证该馆正式著录与学术鉴定文献。
6. 辽宁省博物馆所藏《赤壁图》的归属需要核实。
7. 克利夫兰艺术博物馆是否确实藏有《赵孟頫写经换茶图》？该馆在线目录检索中未确认此作品。
8. 仇英真迹与苏州作坊伪作的系统性区分尚需大量个案研究。Laing 指出许多传世仇英作品的实际归属存疑。

### 社会关系
9. 仇英与唐寅的具体交往情况不详。二人虽同出周臣门下，但唐寅卒于1524年，当时仇英可能还在早期阶段。
10. 仇英在同时代文人笔记中的出现频率和评价如何？王世贞、董其昌之外的明代文献有待系统检索。

### 图像资料
