# Wang Shizhen / 王世贞

## Summary

明代文学家、史学家、官员。后七子领袖之一，嘉靖后期至万历前期文坛盟主。著有《弇州山人四部稿》《弇山堂别集》等。

Ming dynasty writer, historian, and official. Co-leader of the Latter Seven Masters and dominant literary arbiter of late 16th-century China. Author of Yanzhou Shanren Sibu Gao and Yanshantang Bieji.

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Wang Shizhen authored Yanzhou Shanren Sibu Gao in 174 juan, his collected poetry and prose.
- Wang authored Yanshantang Bieji in 100 juan, an unofficial Ming history particularly valuable for supplementing the official Veritable Records on the Jianwen reign.
- Wang authored Jiajing Yilai Shoufu Zhuan in 8 juan, biographies of Grand Secretaries under the Jiajing reign.
