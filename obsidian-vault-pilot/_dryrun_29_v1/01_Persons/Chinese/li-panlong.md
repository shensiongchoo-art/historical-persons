# Li Panlong / 李攀龙

## Summary

明代文学家、后七子领袖之一。与王世贞同为复古文学运动旗手，编有《古今诗删》《唐诗选》。

Ming dynasty writer and co-leader of the Latter Seven Masters. Advocated archaizing literary theory alongside Wang Shizhen. Compiled Gujin Shishan and Tangshi Xuan, influential poetry anthologies.
