# Li Dongyang / 李东阳

## Summary

明代政治家、文学家。字宾之，号西涯，湖广茶陵人。天顺八年进士，官至内阁首辅。茶陵诗派创始人，著有《李东阳集》《怀麓堂集》。卒谥文正。

Ming dynasty statesman and literary leader. Courtesy name Binzhi, art name Xiya. From Chaling, Huguang. Jinshi of 1464, rose to Grand Secretary. Founder of the Chaling literary school. Author of Collected Works of Li Dongyang. Posthumous name Wenzheng.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Li Dongyang was born in 1447 in Chaling, Huguang (modern Hunan).
- Li Dongyang died in 1516 and was posthumously named Wenzheng.
- Li Dongyang's courtesy name was Binzhi and his art name was Xiya.
- Li Dongyang earned the jinshi degree in 1464 at the age of 18.
- Li Dongyang rose to Grand Secretary (1505-1512), serving under the Chenghua, Hongzhi, and Zhengde emperors.
- Li Dongyang was known as a literary leader and founded the Chaling School of poetry.
