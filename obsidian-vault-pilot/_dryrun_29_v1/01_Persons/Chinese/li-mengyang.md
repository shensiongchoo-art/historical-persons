# Li Mengyang / 李梦阳

## Summary

明代文学家，前七子之首。字献吉，号空同。弘治六年进士。倡导文学复古，主张“文必秦汉，诗必盛唐”。著有《空同集》。

Ming dynasty literary figure, leader of the Former Seven Masters. Courtesy name Xianji, art name Kongtong. Jinshi of 1493. Advocated literary archaism. Author of the Kongtong Collection.
