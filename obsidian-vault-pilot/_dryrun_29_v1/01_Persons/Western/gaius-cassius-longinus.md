# Gaius Cassius Longinus / 盖乌斯·卡西乌斯·朗基努斯

## Summary

罗马共和国末期元老、将领。凯撒刺杀行动的主要策划者，伊壁鸠鲁派哲学家。腓立比战役后自杀。

Late Roman Republic senator and general. Leading instigator of Julius Caesar's assassination. Epicurean philosopher. Committed suicide after the Battle of Philippi.
