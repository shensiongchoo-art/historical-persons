# Marcus Tullius Cicero / 马库斯·图利乌斯·西塞罗

## Summary

罗马演说家、政治家、律师、哲学家和作家。公元前63年任执政官期间镇压喀提林阴谋，后遭流放并被召回。著有大量演说词、哲学著作和书信。公元前43年在后三头同盟的公敌宣告中被杀。

Roman orator, statesman, lawyer, philosopher, and writer. Consul in 63 BCE when he suppressed the Catilinarian conspiracy; later exiled and recalled. Prolific author of speeches, philosophical works, and letters. Killed in the proscriptions of 43 BCE.

## Key Historical / Reception / Legend / Fiction Separation

### Confirmed Historical Facts
- Marcus Tullius Cicero was born on 3 January 106 BCE in Arpinum.
- Cicero was killed on 7 December 43 BCE in the proscriptions of the Second Triumvirate.
- Cicero was a 'novus homo' — the first in his family to attain the consulship.
- Cicero served as consul in 63 BCE.
- During his consulship, Cicero suppressed the Catilinarian conspiracy.
- Cicero was exiled in 58 BCE for his actions in the Catilinarian affair.

### Disputed / Uncertain
- The legality of Cicero's execution of the Catilinarian conspirators without trial was disputed and later used against him by political enemies.

### Reception / Later Groupings (not formal organizations)
- Cicero was one of Rome's greatest orators and had a profound influence on Latin prose style.
- Cicero's speeches and letters are among the most important contemporary sources for the late Roman Republic.
- The story of Fulvia piercing Cicero's tongue with a hairpin after his death should be treated as a later legend; its historicity is doubtful.
