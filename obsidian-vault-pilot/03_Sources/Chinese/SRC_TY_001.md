## Notes

Official Ming history compiled under Zhang Tingyu (1672–1755) by imperial order, completed in 1739. The Tang Yin biography is in 文苑传二 (Wenyuan Zhuan, part 2), juan 286. As an official dynastic history, it carries high authority, but was compiled nearly 200 years after Tang Yin's death and reflects Qing-era editorial choices.
