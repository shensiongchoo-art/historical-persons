## Notes

Gaius Suetonius Tranquillus (c. 69–122 CE) wrote the Lives of the Caesars during the reign of Hadrian, approximately 150 years after Caesar's death. As imperial secretary (ab epistulis), Suetonius had access to imperial archives and official documents.

Key chapters:
- Ch. 1: Birth and family (gens Julia, Venus descent)
- Ch. 76: Dictator perpetuo appointment
- Ch. 80–82: Assassination (the most detailed ancient account)

**Source caveat:** Suetonius includes anecdotal, physical, and personal detail alongside historical narrative. His interest in omens, personal habits, and scandal means some material may be embellished. He is however the most detailed source for Caesar's assassination.
