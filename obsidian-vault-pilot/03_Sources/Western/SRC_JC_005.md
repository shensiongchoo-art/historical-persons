## Notes

Cassius Dio (c. 155–235 CE) was a Roman senator of Greek origin who wrote an 80-book history of Rome from its founding to 229 CE. Books 37–44 cover the Caesarian period from 68 to 44 BCE.

Written approximately 200 years after Caesar's death, Cassius Dio synthesizes earlier sources (many lost). His senatorial perspective and distance from events mean his narrative reflects later historical interpretation.

Loeb editions: LCL 53 covers Books 36–40; LCL 66 covers Books 41–45. The text for some books survives only in epitomes (summaries) by Byzantine scholars.
